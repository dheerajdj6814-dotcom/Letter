To My Kannamma ❤️

Kanna… nenu enduku ippudu Hyderabad vachano neeku cheppali. 🥹

Mana discussion lo nuvvu oka maata annav kada… "Ikkada undi unte nuvvu baavundu anipistadi" ani.

Nuvvu exact ga “na pakkana undali” ani cheppakapoyina, aa maata venaka unna feeling naaku ardham ayyindi kanna. Aa time lo nijanga neeku naa presence kavali anipinchindani naaku anipinchindi.

And tarvata nuvvu “nuvvu raaku… guilt tho ninnu face cheyalenu” annappudu… ayyo kanna, aa maata naaku entha elano anipinchindo cheppalenu. 🥺

Manam enni tittukunna, enni kottukunna, chivariki manalni maname face cheskovali kada… at last naku nuvvu, neeku nenu anthe. Inkosari epudu ala anukoku. 🥺 Nuvvu naaku epudaina face cheyaleni person kaadu kanna. Nuvvu eppatiki naatho undalsina person.

Na drustilo nuvvu oka chinna baby… na bujji papa vi. 🥹🤌 Amma ani tittina amma kakunda podhu kada… Alage naa kuthuru em chesina, nanna ki adi muchatagaane untadi, cute gaane anipistadi.

Alage nuvvu kuda na drustilo… konni vishayallo nannu support chesi, guide chesi, correct chese naa amma vi, cute ga aligi, naa tho muchata chese naa kuthuru vi.

Anduke nuvvu em chesina, nenu ninnu alaane chustha. Nuvvu guilt tho na mundu undakudadhu. Naa daggara nuvvu alane undali. ❤️

Nenu ippudu raakunda Friday varaku wait cheste… nee mind lo unna aa feeling Friday varaku carry forward ayyi, nuvvu alane feel avuthavemo ani naaku anipinchindi. Kani ventane kalisi, kasepu nee daggara undi, ninnu manchiga hug cheskunte… avi anni ventane pothayi anipinchindi.

Manam enni kottukunna, enni tittukunna… okasari manushulu pakkana pakkana unte, konni feelings automatic ga pothayi kada. Malli normal aipotham. ❤️

And nijam cheppalante… naaku ninnu chudali ani chaala anipinchindi. Chaala miss ayyanu ninnu. I also missed you a lot nanna, I really wanted to be with you. I did not want to wait till Friday.

Anduke Friday varaku wait cheyyadam kanna, ippude raavadame correct anipinchindi.

So em ekkuva alochinchakunda… ventane vachesa na Kannamma. 🥹❤️🤌
